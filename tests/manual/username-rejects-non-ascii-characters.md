# [Username] Rejects a username containing non-ASCII characters

**Severity:** Minor
**Status:** draft

## Preconditions
User is on the sign-up page.

## Steps
1. Enter "josé123" in the username field
2. Complete the other required fields
3. Submit the form

## Expected Result
The form is not submitted and an error message under the username field states only letters (A-Z, a-z) and numbers are allowed. No account is created.
