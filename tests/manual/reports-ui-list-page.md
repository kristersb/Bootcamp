# [Reports] /reports list page shows all generated reports

**Severity:** Major
**Status:** draft

## Preconditions
At least 2 reports exist, generated from different test runs.

## Steps
1. Navigate to /reports.

## Expected Result
The page lists every report with its suite name, run date, pass/fail/skipped counts and generated date. Clicking a report opens its detail page at /reports/:id.
